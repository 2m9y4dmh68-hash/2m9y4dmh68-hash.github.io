Privacy policy

Metro Empire: Idle Rail Tycoon is developed by Jung Ji Yong (App Studio). Contact: jungjipal369@gmail.com.

The Android game is being prepared for testing. This policy covers the local game features and the optional advertising integration described below. No public production release is currently announced.

1. Local game data

The game is designed to store city progress, coins, train counts, upgrades, reward history and language preferences locally on your device. No game account, cloud game database or developer-operated synchronization server is required. Device or operating-system backup behavior may depend on your settings.

2. Optional advertising

Versions with rewarded advertising use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK and its consent tools. The SDK may collect and share IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, product interactions and diagnostic data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. We do not describe an advertising-enabled version as collecting no data.

Where required, privacy messages explain the available choices before advertising requests. Advertising availability can depend on your region, consent choices and network. The game is designed to remain playable when advertising is unavailable. See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising controls.

3. Choices, retention and deletion

You can change the interface language in Settings. Local progress is retained until cleared through the game, removed through device app storage settings or deleted on uninstall, subject to device backups. Advertising providers manage data according to their own policies. Use the privacy options offered in advertising-enabled versions or your device's advertising controls to review available choices.

4. Support messages

If you email us, we receive the message, your email address and any information you choose to send. This is used to answer your request and resolve the reported issue. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Contact the same address to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable retention obligations.

5. Children and audience

The planned release is intended for a general teen and adult audience and is not directed to children under 13. Applicable age and consent requirements vary by location. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. Contact us if you believe a child has provided information to us.

6. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information under its own privacy statement. The language preview stores its selection in your browser. Fonts and images are hosted with the site; no third-party analytics or advertising script is included on these pages.

7. Changes

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The revision date appears above. Before submitting an advertising-enabled build, we will verify the SDK settings and store disclosures against the actual build.
